Lecture 12

Cuts, Cauchy integrals and causality

What this lecture does

A cut specifies the domain of a branch and separates boundary values of a Cauchy integral. We deform an integration segment to continue that integral across a cut. We then apply a boundary formula to a causal response, with the decay and regularity needed for its real and imaginary parts.

Summary

  • A branch includes its domain and argument range. The quadratic root is a product of principal factors, extended across their common cut.
  • An L1L^1L1 density defines a holomorphic Cauchy integral off its support. A permitted lower deformation gives FII=F+2πiρF_{\mathrm{II}}=F+2\pi\ii\rhoFII​=F+2πiρ below the cut.
  • The scalar resonance zero has a negative imaginary part at order g2g^2g2. Its spectral derivation is not asserted here; Part II develops the model.
  • The assumptions G,tG,G′∈L1G,tG,G'\in L^1G,tG,G′∈L1 give the boundary regularity and upper-half-plane decay needed for the dispersion relations.
