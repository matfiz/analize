Lecture 8

Tempered distributions and Green’s functions on the line

What this lecture does

A point source is described by its action on smooth functions rather than by pointwise values. We define that action, extend differentiation and the Fourier transform to it, and prove the boundary-value formula for a simple pole. We then solve two differential equations with a point source.

Summary

  • Tempered distributions are linear functionals controlled by finitely many Schwartz seminorms. Their pairing is bilinear.
  • Distributional differentiation gives θ′=δ\theta'=\deltaθ′=δ and (ln⁡∣x∣)′=PV⁡(1/x)(\ln\abs x)'=\PV(1/x)(ln∣x∣)′=PV(1/x).
