LpL^pLp spaces, convolution and smoothing

What this lecture does

A wavefunction in L2L^2L2 is specified up to changes on a null set. We prove that limits in integral norm remain in LpL^pLp. We then approximate these classes by smooth functions whose supports stay inside the given open set. These results supply the function spaces used in Fourier analysis and quantum mechanics.
