Lecture 21

Jacobi functions and the pendulum

What this lecture does

The pendulum period is an elliptic integral. Its motion is described by the inverse integral, a Jacobi function. We construct the real Jacobi functions by an ordinary differential equation, prove their periods and verify the pendulum formula with its initial conditions. The limit of these librations is a separatrix motion with infinite period.
