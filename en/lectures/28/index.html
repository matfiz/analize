Lecture 28

Airy functions, Stokes lines and WKB

What this lecture does

The Airy function connects exponential decay on one real side to oscillation on the other. We compute the two oscillatory saddle contributions and derive the formal WKB amplitudes. A simple turning point reduces locally to the Airy model. The stationary-phase input and the general matching argument retain their sketch qualifications.

28.2 Equal-phase and equal-magnitude rays

On the slit −π<arg⁡z<π-\pi<\arg z<\pi−π<argz<π, define ζ(z)=2z3/2/3\zeta(z)=2z^{3/2}/3ζ(z)=2z3/2/3 using the principal power. The formal Airy exponentials are e−ζe^{-\zeta}e−ζ and eζe^\zetaeζ. We call Im⁡ζ=0\Im\zeta=0Imζ=0 the Stokes rays, meaning equal imaginary exponents, and Re⁡ζ=0\Re\zeta=0Reζ=0 the anti-Stokes rays, meaning equal magnitudes. Writing z=reiαz=re^{\ii\alpha}z=reiα gives ζ=(2/3)r3/2e3iα/2\zeta=(2/3)r^{3/2}e^{3\ii\alpha/2}ζ=(2/3)r3/2e3iα/2. Consequently the Stokes rays have arguments 000 and ±2π/3\pm2\pi/3±2π/3. The anti-Stokes rays have arguments ±π/3\pm\pi/3±π/3 and the two boundary values on the negative axis. The latter represent the same geometric ray. The rays are shown below.

The solid rays have equal imaginary exponents. The dashed rays have equal exponential magnitudes. The negative-axis label denotes boundary values of the chosen power, not a branch of the entire Airy function.

The Stokes phenomenon is a change in a sectorial asymptotic description when a subdominant exponential contribution becomes relevant. A subdominant exponential has smaller modulus than its partner in that sector. The exact function Ai⁡\operatorname{Ai}Ai has no jump across any ray: it is entire. The ray calculation alone determines neither a connection multiplier nor a full sectorial asymptotic theorem. Those global results are cited to Ablowitz–Fokas, §6.6; none is needed for the real matching below.

Summary

  • Two sketched stationary-phase contributions give an additive negative-Airy leading sine with envelope π−1/2x−1/4\pi^{-1/2}x^{-1/4}π−1/2x−1/4.
  • Our Stokes convention is Im⁡ζ=0\Im\zeta=0Imζ=0; anti-Stokes means Re⁡ζ=0\Re\zeta=0Reζ=0. The exact entire Airy function does not jump across these rays.
  • Eikonal and transport balance give WKB leading amplitudes. Their displayed normalised differential residual is exact away from zeros of QQQ.
  • The sketched simple-zero connection has amplitude factor 222 and phase π/4\pi/4π/4. The linear-action matching overlap is ε2/3≪∣t∣≪ε2/5\varepsilon^{2/3}\ll|t|\ll\varepsilon^{2/5}ε2/3≪∣t∣≪ε2/5.
