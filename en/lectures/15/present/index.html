Lecture 15

Möbius maps

Fractional-linear maps describe all holomorphic automorphisms of the disk and the upper half-plane. We first describe their action on the sphere. The Schwarz lemma then reduces the disk classification to rotations.

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • Compute a Möbius map at finite points and at infinity.
  • Use the cross ratio to specify the images of three points.
  • Prove the Schwarz lemma, including its equality cases.
  • Classify the holomorphic automorphisms of the disk and half-plane.

15.2 The cross ratio

For distinct finite points define their cross ratio by [z1,z2;z3,z4]=(z1−z3)(z2−z4)(z1−z4)(z2−z3).[z_1,z_2;z_3,z_4] =\frac{(z_1-z_3)(z_2-z_4)}{(z_1-z_4)(z_2-z_3)}.[z1​,z2​;z3​,z4​]=(z1​−z4​)(z2​−z3​)(z1​−z3​)(z2​−z4​)​. When one point is infinity, take the corresponding limit. The order of the four points matters. For instance, [0,1;2,3]=(−2)(−2)/((−3)(−1))=4/3[0,1;2,3]=(-2)(-2)/((-3)(-1))=4/3[0,1;2,3]=(−2)(−2)/((−3)(−1))=4/3.

Proposition 15.2. Every Möbius map preserves the cross ratio, using limits when a point or its image is infinity. Given two ordered triples of distinct sphere points, there is exactly one Möbius map taking the first triple to the second.

Proof. At ordinary finite points subtraction gives Tg(z)−Tg(v)=(αδ−βγ)(z−v)(γz+δ)(γv+δ).T_g(z)-T_g(v) =\frac{(\alpha\delta-\beta\gamma)(z-v)} {(\gamma z+\delta)(\gamma v+\delta)}.Tg​(z)−Tg​(v)=(γz+δ)(γv+δ)(αδ−βγ)(z−v)​. Insert this expression in the cross ratio. Each determinant and each linear denominator cancels. Limits prove the exceptional cases.

For a triple (a,b,c)(a,b,c)(a,b,c), the map Qa,b,c(z)=(z−b)(c−a)(z−a)(c−b)Q_{a,b,c}(z)=\frac{(z-b)(c-a)}{(z-a)(c-b)}Qa,b,c​(z)=(z−a)(c−b)(z−b)(c−a)​ takes (a,b,c)(a,b,c)(a,b,c) to (∞,0,1)(\infty,0,1)(∞,0,1). Limits give the same construction if one point is infinity. Its numerator and denominator have distinct zeros, so its determinant is nonzero. Composing two such maps, with one inverted, gives existence for arbitrary triples. A Möbius map fixing infinity has γ=0\gamma=0γ=0. If it also fixes zero and one, its formula is z↦zz\mapsto zz↦z. Conjugating by the constructed maps gives uniqueness. 

The triple construction has a useful real version. If all three points in each triple lie in R∪{∞}\R\cup\{\infty\}R∪{∞}, the displayed formulas have real coefficients. Thus the interpolating map has a real matrix, up to an overall scalar. This consequence will identify the half-plane automorphisms.

To construct a map between prescribed triples, first send the source triple to the standard triple used in the proof. Send the target triple to that same standard triple by a second map. Apply the first map. Then apply the inverse of the second. Substituting each prescribed point checks the order of composition, including any value at infinity.

15.3 The Schwarz lemma

Write D={z∈C:∣z∣<1}\DD=\{z\in\C:|z|<1\}D={z∈C:∣z∣<1} for the unit disk. A bijective holomorphic map with holomorphic inverse is called biholomorphic. An automorphism of a domain is a biholomorphic map from the domain to itself. We first control a holomorphic self-map with a fixed point at zero.

Summary

  • Determinant-one matrices act on the sphere, with kernel {I,−I}\{I,-I\}{I,−I}. Möbius maps preserve the stated ordered cross ratio.
  • Schwarz bounds a disk self-map fixing zero by ∣f(z)∣≤∣z∣|f(z)|\le|z|∣f(z)∣≤∣z∣. Equality away from zero, or ∣f′(0)∣=1|f'(0)|=1∣f′(0)∣=1, forces a rotation.
  • Every disk automorphism is eiθbae^{\ii\theta}b_aeiθba​. Every half-plane automorphism has a real determinant-one matrix.

The Cayley map transfers the two classifications to one another.
