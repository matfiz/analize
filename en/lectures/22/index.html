Lecture 22

Theta functions and Poisson summation

What this lecture does

Summing a rapidly decreasing function over integer translates produces a periodic function. Its Fourier coefficients prove Poisson summation, the result deferred from Lecture 7. Applied to a Gaussian, it gives the theta modular law. We also prove quasi-periodicity, count the zeros and derive the heat equation in the same normalisation.

22.3 One simple zero per cell
