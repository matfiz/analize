Lecture 11

Singularities and residues

An isolated singularity contributes one Laurent coefficient to a contour integral. We prove this residue formula and use it to evaluate real integrals. The limiting contours require estimates as well as residues.

By the end of this lecture you will be able to:

  • Expand a holomorphic function on an annulus and classify an isolated singularity.
  • Evaluate residues and justify semicircle and keyhole limits.
  • Count zeros minus poles with multiplicities by the argument principle.
  • Characterise meromorphic functions on the Riemann sphere.

Summary

  • A holomorphic function on an annulus has a unique Laurent expansion. Its coefficient of power −1-1−1 is the residue.
